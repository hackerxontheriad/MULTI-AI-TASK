Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

1. Information processed

When you sign in, the server validates your Google identity token and stores a minimal identity (Google subject identifier, email, display name) in an encrypted, HttpOnly session cookie. Chat prompts and responses are sent to the model provider selected for the request. Some routes use official provider APIs, while optional community adapters may be experimental and may process prompts under their own terms.

2. Chat history

The current browser keeps chat history in session storage for this browser tab. People with access to that browser profile may be able to see it. Closing the tab or clearing site data can remove this session-local history. Server-side chat history sync is not currently provided.

3. Third-party processing

AI requests may be routed through third-party community providers; do not submit passwords, API keys, financial details, confidential business data, or sensitive personal information. Google Sign-In and Google Drive are subject to Google’s policies.

4. Retention and security

Session cookies expire within the configured lifetime. Security controls include HTTPS cookie settings, server-side Google token validation, and security response headers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices

You may sign out, clear local browser storage, revoke Google permissions in your Google Account, or stop using the service. Contact the service operator for data requests.

6. Children and policy updates

This experimental service is not designed for children under 13, or the higher minimum age required by local law. The operator should add jurisdiction-specific notices, an effective date, and a working privacy contact before public launch.
