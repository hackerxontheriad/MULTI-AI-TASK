Expected limitations
- Official API keys may incur charges, and provider model IDs/availability can change.
- Community g4f adapters are unofficial; they may be offline, require a session/browser or fail after upstream changes.
- `Auto` is one call into g4f's Auto route. Any internal behavior within that upstream library is outside the gateway's control; choosing an exact provider row offers stricter routing control.
- Image and video routes are experimental. They return errors instead of silently trying another model or fabricating output.
